Marathon Update 1.1.5.2

The one about more Vault Breaker

Marathon Dev Team August 4, 2026
updates327 words1 min read

Vault Breaker

  • We have extended the duration of the Vault Breaker mode!
    • New end date: August 18.
    • Increased the available stock of some limited offers to reflect this extension.
    • We look forward to continuing to hear your feedback on this experimental PvE mode.
  • Updated the Key Template Armory reward to correctly specify that it only awards Cryo Archive keys.

Item Economy

  • Sponsored Kit Boosts - Free Sponsored Kits have now been upgraded:
    • Starting weapon now come with random Enhanced (green) mods.
    • 1 stack of Enhanced (green) Patch Kits.
    • 1 random grenade (smoke, heat, or claymore).

Combat

Weapons

  • V22 Volt Thrower
    • Damage increased from 12.6 to 14.4.
      • Developer’s Note: This is a partial revert of the damage reduction we applied on 7/24, which had brought Volt Thrower damage down from 18 to 12.6.
    • Tracking speed will now slowly decay over 1.2 seconds of a projectile's lifetime by a maximum of 30%.
    • Reduced the amount of time spent at 540rpm before weapon heat starts to spin down by ~20%.
  • KKV-9SD
    • Increased base magazine size from 23 to 28.
      • Retuned magazine sizes at every stat value to compensate.
      • Prestige Flechette Drum magazine mod stays the same at 87.
    • Fixed an issue where certain magazine mods were not correctly granting any additional rounds in the magazine.
  • V85 Circuit Breaker
    • Fixed a stability issue relating to the Prestige quality Overlocked Delimiter mod.
      • This mod has now been re-enabled.

Zones

  • Fixed an issue where opening the Cryo Hatch repeatedly could grant unintended amounts of faction reputation and Season XP.
    • This action will now provide the intended gains once per match.
    • All actions that had previously had their reputation and XP gains disabled in Cryo Archive have now been re-enabled.

General

  • Fixed an issue where players would sometimes not receive Salvage or Schemas when purchasing Rewards Pass items with LUX.
  • Fixed an issue with the Store where some bundle items that should have also been visible in the Catalog section were not displaying correctly.

破库者

  • 我们延长了破库者模式的持续时间！
    • 新的结束日期：8月18日。
    • 为了配合此次延长，我们增加了部分限定优惠的库存。
    • 我们期待大家能继续对这一实验性PvE模式进行反馈。
  • 更新了钥匙模板军械库奖励，使其正确显示为仅奖励低温档案室钥匙。

物品经济

  • 赞助包加成——免费赞助包现已升级：
    • 初始武器现在会随机附带强化（绿色）模组。
    • 1层强化（绿色）修补包。
    • 1个随机手雷（烟雾、热量或阔刀地雷）。

战斗

武器

  • V22伏特放射器
    • 伤害从12.6提升至14.4。
      • 开发者提示：这是对7月24日实施的伤害削减的部分回调，之前的削减将伏特放射器的伤害从18降低到了12.6。
    • 追踪速度现在会在射弹的1.2秒内缓慢衰减，最多衰减30%。
    • 减少了武器达到540射速时因过热而导致转速下降的时间约20%。
  • KKV-9SD
    • 基础弹匣容量从23增加到28。
      • 重新调整了所有属性值的弹匣容量以进行补偿。
      • 尊享飞镖鼓式弹匣模组仍保持为87。
    • 修复了某些弹匣模组无法正确增加弹匣容量的问题。
  • V85断路器
    • 修复了与尊享品质的超频限定器模组相关的稳定性问题。
      • 该模组现已重新启用。

区域

  • 修复了反复开启低温舱门可能获得意外数量的阵营声望和赛季经验值的问题。
    • 现在，此操作每场比赛只会获得一次预期收益。
    • 所有之前低温档案室无法获取声望和经验值的操作现已重新启用。

综合

  • 修复了玩家有时使用奢享币购买奖励通票物品时无法获得回收资源或方案的问题。
  • 修复了商店中某些本应在目录部分显示的组合包物品显示不正确的问题。
