Marathon Update 1.1.9.7

The one about Ordnance Heist returning

Marathon Dev Team November 10, 2026
updates274 words1 min read

Events

  • Sponsored Night Marsh will be available this weekend, starting Thursday, November 12.
  • Sponsored Survival: Perimeter will be available all week until November 17.

Ordnance Heist

  • Ordnance Heist is back! The event will be available from November 10 - November 24.
  • MIDA Armory
    • Deluxe sponsored kit: Stock decreased from 3/week to 2/week.
    • Superior sponsored kit: No longer offered.
    • MIDA themed weapon: Stock decreased from 2/week to 1/week and rarity decreased from Superior to Deluxe.
    • Sneak Backpack: Rarity decreased from Superior to Deluxe.
    • Synapse Cube: Stock decreased from 1/week to 1 total.
  • Traxus Armory
    • Deluxe sponsored kit: Stock decreased from 3/week to 2/week.
    • Superior sponsored kit: No longer offered.
    • Traxus themed weapon: Stock decreased from 2/week to 1/week and rarity decreased from Superior to Deluxe.
    • Ammo Backpack: Rarity decreased from Superior to Deluxe.
    • Synapse Cube: Stock decreased from 1/week to 1 total.
  • Economy Changes
    • Firestorm key templates will only grant 1 key upon completion.
    • Firestorm arms lockers now only require 1 key to open.
    • The Firestorm will have a 15% chance to drop from supply drops prior to and during the event.
      • After the event, it will have a 30% drop rate.

Zones

  • Fixed an issue on Outpost where players could die in a location that left their loot bags inaccessible.
  • Fixed an issue in Vault 4 of Cryo Archive where players could fall into a particular spot and get stuck.

General

  • Updated Report options on player profiles.
  • Fixed an issue where using a consumable as an overheat weapon animation begins would cause a bright flash to appear on screen.
  • Fixed an issue where Stickers were not properly displaying when applied with NuCal weapon styles.

活动

  • 赞助夜间沼泽将于本周末上线，11月13日周五开始。
  • 赞助生存：外围区域将整周开放，直至11月18日。

军械盗劫

  • 军械盗劫现已回归！活动将于11月11日至11月25日期间开放。
  • 米达军械库
    • 豪华赞助包：库存从每周3件减少到每周2件。
    • 卓越赞助包：不再提供。
    • 米达主题武器：库存从每周2件减少到每周1件，稀有度从卓越降至豪华。
    • 潜行包背包：稀有度从卓越降至豪华。
    • 突触立方：库存从每周1件减少到总共1件。
  • 特莱克斯军械库
    • 豪华赞助包：库存从每周3件减少到每周2件。
    • 卓越赞助包：不再提供。
    • 特莱克斯主题武器：库存从每周2件减少到每周1件，稀有度从卓越降至豪华。
    • 弹药背包：稀有度从卓越降至豪华。
    • 突触立方：库存从每周1件减少到总共1件。
  • 经济改动
    • 完成烈焰风暴钥匙模板后，仅会获得1把钥匙。
    • 开启烈焰风暴武器柜现在仅需1把钥匙。
    • 在活动前及活动期间，烈焰风暴将有15%的几率从补给投放中掉落。
      • 活动结束后，其掉率将变为30%。

区域

  • 修复了前哨中，玩家可能会在某些位置死亡，而其掉落的战利品包无法被拾取的问题。
  • 修复了低温档案室4号保险库中，玩家可能会掉入特定地点并被卡住的问题。

综合

  • 更新了玩家个人资料中的举报选项。
  • 修复了在武器过热动画开始时使用消耗品，会导致屏幕上出现强烈闪光的问题。
  • 修复了在使用纽卡洛里武器风格时，贴纸无法正常显示的问题。
