Nightfall Refresh and December Update Symbiosis

We previously shared plans for Marathon’s next major update to launch on September 22. We know many players were looking forward to it, and we appreciate your patience. We are adjusting our plans to ensure the update meets your expectations.

The content originally planned for September will now arrive as part of our Symbiosis update on December 8. Alongside the new Runner shell, permanent PvE experience, and refreshed Perimeter zone, the update will also include a social space/firing range, an experimental TDM mode, and improvements to new player onboarding.

Before then, we’ll release a smaller, focused update on October 6 that resets progression and the in-game economy, refreshes rewards, and improves the live game, while giving the team the time needed to deliver the larger experiences planned for December and March.

We’re also moving away from a strict seasonal schedule. Instead of tying major updates to fixed seasonal deadlines, we’re giving the team more flexibility to build, test, and improve features before they are released.

Since launch, you’ve told us Marathon needs more depth, more variety, stronger progression, and better reasons to keep playing over the long term. We agree. Addressing that feedback requires us to change both what we’re building and how we deliver it.

October Nightfall Refresh

On October 6, we’ll reset progression and the in-game economy, giving all players a fresh starting point.

Marathon Season 2: Nightfall launched with progression and reward issues that accelerated some players into the endgame while leaving others behind. This made Extraction more difficult to engage with and prevented the reset from working as intended. October gives us an opportunity to apply what we learned and establish a healthier progression experience.

The October update will include:

  • A progression and economy reset
  • A new Rewards Pass
  • Quality-of-life improvements
  • Live tuning and balance updates

Vault Breaker will also return for a limited time, giving us additional data about how PvE fits into progression a few weeks after a reset. That feedback will help shape the future of PvE in Marathon.

In the 8 weeks between October and December, we’ll continue running gameplay experiments and sharing what we’re learning, including where player feedback is influencing development.

December Symbiosis

Symbiosis will be the first major step in Marathon’s next chapter, launching on December 8th.

The update will introduce Marathon’s first permanent PvE experience, providing a new way to explore, progress, and engage with the world beyond traditional PvPvE extraction gameplay. Symbiosis is an important milestone for Marathon and the beginning of a broader evolution through March and beyond, shaped by what we learn from players along the way.

Symbiosis will also include a refreshed Perimeter experience, a new social space with a firing range, a new Runner shell, an experimental PvP Team Deathmatch mode, and the first of several improvements to the new-player experience.

The Path Ahead

October will refresh the live game and give players a healthier starting point. Symbiosis will be a significant milestone for Marathon, introducing permanent PvE and several new ways to engage with the game. But it’s only the beginning of what we’re working toward. As players spend time with these updates and share their feedback, we’ll continue refining and expanding Marathon.

We’ll share more about that future through Dev Insights videos and regular development updates. Our first blog about Marathon’s future plans is scheduled for later this month where we will share how we will be evolving Marathon into a more alien and dynamic survival experience.

We know you’ll have questions, and we’ll answer them as clearly and quickly as we can. We’ll continue showing our work and providing regular updates as we move toward Symbiosis and the future beyond it.

Your feedback continues to play an important role in shaping Marathon. Thank you for being part of that process.

Talk soon.

暮夜刷新与12月更新共生

我们之前分享过，《失落星船：马拉松》下一次重大更新计划将于9月23日上线。我们知道很多玩家都期待这次更新，感谢大家的耐心等待。我们正在调整计划，以确保更新能够满足大家的期望。

原计划在9月推出的内容现在将在12月9日的共生更新中推出。除了全新疾行者外壳、永久性PvE体验和外围区域刷新区域外，本次更新还将包括一个社交空间/射击场、一个实验性的团队死亡竞赛模式，以及对新手引导的改进。

在此之前，我们将于10月7日发布一个有针对性的较小更新，重置游戏进度和游戏内经济系统，更新奖励，并改进实时游戏体验，同时让团队有时间开发计划于12月和3月推出的大型更新内容。

我们也将不再采用严格的赛季制。我们不再将重大更新与固定的赛季截止日期挂钩，而是让团队有更大的灵活性，在发布前构建、测试和改进功能。

自游戏上线以来，你们一直告诉我们，《失落星船：马拉松》的内容需要更深刻、更多样，进度系统需要更完善，这样才能让玩家长期玩下去。我们对此表示赞同。为了回应这些反馈，我们需要改变当前的开发内容以及发布方式。

10月暮夜刷新

10月7日，我们将重置游戏进度和游戏内经济系统，为所有玩家提供一个全新的起点。

《失落星船：马拉松》第2赛季：暮夜上线时存在进度和奖励方面的问题，导致部分玩家过早地进入了终局内容，而其他玩家则被远远甩在了后面。这使得撤离难度增加，重置也无法正常运作。我们将利用10月这个机会，应用吸取到的经验，建立更良好的进度体验。

10月更新内容包括：

  • 进度和经济系统重置
  • 全新奖励通票
  • 游戏体验改善
  • 实时调整和平衡更新

破库者也将限时回归，这样我们就能收集更多数据，了解重置几周后PvE在进度系统中的情况。这些反馈将能帮助我们在《失落星船：马拉松》中构建PvE的未来。

在10月至12月的8周内，我们将继续进行游戏实验，并分享发现，包括玩家反馈对游戏开发的影响。

12月共生

共生将于12月8日上线，这也标志着《失落星船：马拉松》新篇章的开启。

此次更新将引入《失落星船：马拉松》首个永久性PvE体验，超越传统的PvPvE撤离玩法，为玩家提供探索、发展和参与游戏世界的新方式。共生是《失落星船：马拉松》发展历程中的一个重要里程碑，也是我们3月之后全面进化的开端，我们将根据玩家的反馈不断调整。

共生还将包含更新的外围区域体验、带有射击场的全新社交空间、全新的疾行者外壳、实验性PvP团队死亡竞赛模式，以及一系列针对新手体验的改进。

未来道路

10月我们将对游戏进行全面更新，为玩家提供一个更良好的起点。共生更是《失落星船：马拉松》的一个重要里程碑，它将引入永久性的PvE模式以及多种全新游戏方式。但这仅仅是我们努力的开始。随着玩家体验这些更新并分享反馈，我们将继续完善和扩展《失落星船：马拉松》。

我们将通过开发者见解视频和定期开发更新，分享更多未来的信息。本月晚些时候，我们会发布第一篇关于《失落星船：马拉松》未来计划的博文，届时将分享如何让《失落星船：马拉松》发展成为一个更新颖异域、更动态的生存体验。

知道大家会有疑问，我们会尽可能清晰及时地解答。在开发共生和未来内容的过程中，我们将继续向大家跟进我们的工作，并定期发布更新。

一直以来，你们的反馈对于《失落星船：马拉松》的开发至关重要。感谢你们的参与。

下次再见。
