Marathon Update 1.1.9.6

The one about the rogue dropship

Marathon Dev Team October 27, 2026
community51 words1 min read

Events

  • Sponsored Perimeter will be available this weekend, starting Thursday, 10/29.

Contracts

  • Fixed an issue where players were not receiving the intended bonus reputation rewards when completing certain contracts solo or in duos.

Zones

  • Fixed an issue on Night Marsh where players could get downed by an improperly spawning UESC dropship.

活动

  • 赞助外围区域将于本周末上线，10月30日周五开始。

合约

  • 修复了玩家在单人或双人完成特定合约时，未能获得预期额外声望奖励的问题。

区域

  • 修复了在夜间沼泽中，玩家可能因UESC运输舰生成位置异常而被击倒的问题。
