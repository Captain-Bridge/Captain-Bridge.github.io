The Future of Marathon

What’s next for Marathon: Nightfall Refresh in October, Symbiosis in December, and more in March.

Del Chafe III and Julia Nardin September 24, 2026
community1603 words7 min read

Hey everyone, thanks for checking in.

Since launch, we’ve been reviewing your feedback and taking a hard look at where Marathon needs to go next. We’ve heard from new players who found the game difficult to understand, experienced players who wanted greater clarity about its future, and players who enjoyed what we built but wanted more reasons to keep coming back.

We know you expect to hear from us consistently about where Marathon is headed. Today, we want to share more about what the team is building and the vision guiding Marathon’s evolution into 2027.

Our next steps begin with the Nightfall Refresh in October, expand significantly with Symbiosis in December, and take another large leap forward with Marathon’s evolution in March.

  • October: A refresh to progression, rewards, and the live experience.
  • December: Symbiosis introduces Marathon’s first permanent PvE experience, an expanded Perimeter, a new shell, improved onboarding, and a social space.
  • March: These pieces come together through connected PvE, PvP, and PvEvP experiences, deeper progression, a refactored Cradle, more narrative, new enemies, and an entirely new zone.

And if you haven’t watched or read Josh and Poria’s Next Chapter for Bungie update, we recommend taking a look. What follows is Marathon’s part of that larger conversation.

Growing beyond Extraction

Marathon launched as a cutthroat, winner-takes-all PvEvP experience. We want to preserve that thrill for the players who enjoy it, while creating more ways for others to experience the world and find the level of challenge that works for them.

Disclaimer: The above is a still from an upcoming Symbiosis CG Trailer.

Some players want a quick, lower-pressure run after work. Some want to spend an entire night with their crew. Others want to test a build, compete directly against other players, chase a difficult challenge, or follow a strange clue deeper into the ruins of Tau Ceti IV.

Marathon needs to support all of them.

As we build from Symbiosis toward March and beyond, we’re focused on three areas.

More ways to play

PvEvP will remain an important part of Marathon, but it will become one point in a connected set of survival experiences.

We’re building toward:

  • Connected PvE, PvP, and PvEvP experiences
  • Different levels of intensity and commitment
  • Options for solo players and crews
  • More approachable ways to enter and learn the game
  • Experiences built around cooperation, competition, and high-risk survival

More approachable entry points will help new and returning players understand Marathon’s systems and build confidence before taking on its highest-stakes challenges. At the same time, experienced players will have more opportunities to test their mastery and pursue deeper challenges.

Your arsenal of shells

You play as a Runner, a bio-cybernetic mercenary whose consciousness can be re-uploaded into a new shell after death. Over time, you’ll build an arsenal of shells that reflects your individual aesthetic and playstyle.

Every run should reinforce the thrill of adaptation as you become stronger and more capable, refining your build to reenter the fight as the version of yourself that the moment demands. We want to rethink how Runners interact with their Cradle and give you more agency over each shell’s progression, capabilities, and its specialized role in your arsenal.

A world that consumes you

At its heart, Marathon is about what happens when humanity pushes into the unknown and the unknown pushes back.

Tau Ceti IV should feel more alien and hostile, with stronger connections between our game and the original Marathon trilogy. We want its world to reward curiosity and risk-taking, with more narrative that expands your understanding of the universe across repeated runs.

It also means more aliens to fight and more memorable encounters to take on with your crew. It’s not a Marathon game unless you’re shooting the Pfhor... even if they’ve undergone a transformation of their own since the fandom saw them last.

We’re developing new, more spectacular combatants designed to challenge how you work together. Whether you’re confronting something massive for the first time or fighting your way out after a run goes sideways, we want Tau Ceti IV to deliver experiences you and your friends will still be talking about after you extract.

And Tau Ceti IV still has more of itself to reveal. We’re building an entirely new zone that will take Runners deeper into the unknown, but we’ll save what’s lurking there for another time.

October: Nightfall Refresh

Earlier this month, we announced that we’re moving away from strict seasonal releases. That means setting the pace of future updates based on what we’re building, the size of the work, and what the team can responsibly support rather than allowing the calendar alone to determine when something ships.

We’re kicking this off with the Nightfall Refresh in early October. It’s a focused update designed to improve the current experience while the team continues building the larger additions coming in December and March.

Details on what Nightfall Refresh will include: https://x.com/MarathonDevTeam/status/2100253598609535344

December: Symbiosis

Our first significant step into the future arrives with Symbiosis in December.

Symbiosis adds more ways to play, more approachable ways to learn the game, and more opportunities to explore Tau Ceti IV in lower-pressure environments. The experiences introduced in December are just the start and will give us new foundations to expand and connect as we build toward March.

A dedicated PvE experience

You’ve been clear that Marathon needs a place where players can explore, experiment, and take on challenges without worrying about being hunted by other Runners.

In response, Symbiosis will introduce Marathon’s first permanent PvE mode. We’re still finalizing parts of the experience and will share a deeper look before the update launches.

A new way to compete

Symbiosis will also introduce an experimental Team Deathmatch mode. This experience offers a more direct, lower-commitment way to sharpen your skills and compete without putting a full run at risk. We’ll be watching how players engage with the experiment and using what we learn to help shape the future of competitive play in Marathon.

An expanded Perimeter

Perimeter has served as an entry point for new Runners since launch, but it’s lacked the distinct identity and depth of our other zones.

In Symbiosis, Perimeter has undergone a significant visual overhaul. A new fungal ecosystem is spreading across the landscape, transforming familiar spaces, opening new routes and giving the zone its own unique look and feel that sets it apart from our other zones.

Perimeter has also been missing the kinds of experiences found at locations like Pinwheel on Outpost and the Upper Complex on Night Marsh, which give players meaningful challenges to overcome and high-tier loot for them to earn.

Symbiosis brings that same sense of discovery and accomplishment to Perimeter through new points of interest and challenging summits to climb, including a pinnacle encounter that features our largest combatant yet: the Tick Queen.

A new shell

As far as shell archetypes go, we’re also adding a new one to your roster. Bishop: Marathon’s high-performance robot samurai.

Bishop is a fast, surgical hunter-killer built around speed, surprise, and devastating bursts of melee damage. How that gameplay fantasy fits into your crew, and the role Bishop can play across Marathon’s different experiences, is something we look forward to seeing players discover for themselves.

A better way into Marathon

Marathon currently asks new players to learn too much, too quickly.

We’re improving the onboarding experience to create a smoother onramp into Marathon’s world so that new players can more easily learn the fundamentals and build confidence before pushing deeper into Tau Ceti IV.

As part of this work, we’re also adding a CyAc-sponsored social space with a built-in firing range where players will be able to meet, prepare, test weapons and abilities, and get comfortable with their shell under ONI’s watchful eye.

March: Marathon’s evolution

In March, these pieces will come together as part of a much larger evolution of Marathon.

Our plans for this update include:

  • An interconnected system of permanent PvE, PvP, and PvEvP experiences
  • A new onboarding experience
  • A refactored Cradle and deeper shell progression
  • More focused questlines and narrative
  • New alien combatants
  • An entirely new zone

March represents something meaningfully larger than Symbiosis, but it also isn’t the end of Marathon’s evolution. The greatest joy of working on a live service game is developing it alongside you, our players. March is the point where the experience we’ve described begins to operate as a connected whole, giving us a stronger foundation to build upon beyond 2027.

And we’ll do it together.

Evolving with intention

We know that rebuilding trust requires more than talking about the future. We need to share our work and clearly communicate what’s changing so the Marathon community has an accurate understanding of what each update will and won’t deliver.

While we build toward December and March, we’ll continue making meaningful quality-of-life improvements and running focused experiments that allow players to participate without reorganizing their lives around limited windows.

Alongside this blog, we’re also releasing a new Dev Insights video that takes a closer look at some of the challenges we’re working through and the spaces we’re building. Rather than put out a traditional development diary, we wanted to give you all an honest look at what we’re trying to solve and the decisions shaping our direction as the work comes together inside the studio.

We still have a lot to do, and we’ll have more to share as each part of the experience takes shape.

For now, thank you for playing, for giving us clear and passionate feedback, and for sticking with us as we build Marathon into the game we know it can become.

-Del and Julia

大家好，感谢前来查看。

自上线以来，我们一直在审视大家的反馈，并认真思考 Marathon 接下来该走向何方。我们听到了新玩家的声音，他们觉得游戏难以理解；也听到了老玩家的声音，他们希望更清楚地了解游戏的未来；还有那些喜欢我们所打造内容、但希望有更多理由持续回归的玩家。

我们知道，大家期望我们持续分享 Marathon 的前进方向。今天，我们想进一步分享团队正在打造的内容，以及指引 Marathon 迈向 2027 年演进的愿景。

我们的下一步将从 10 月的 Nightfall Refresh 开始，在 12 月随着 Symbiosis 大幅扩展，并在 3 月随着 Marathon 的演进再次迈出一大步。

  • 10 月：对进度、奖励和线上体验进行焕新。
  • 12 月：Symbiosis 将带来 Marathon 首个永久 PvE 体验、扩展后的 Perimeter、全新外壳、改进的新手引导，以及一个 社交 空间。
  • 3 月：这些内容将通过相互关联的 PvE、PvP 和 PvEvP 体验汇聚起来，包括更深入的进度系统、重构后的 摇篮、更多叙事内容、新敌人，以及一个全新区域。

如果你还没有观看或阅读 Josh 和 Poria 的《Bungie 下一篇章》更新，我们建议你看一看。以下内容是 Marathon 在这场更大对话中的部分。

超越撤离

Marathon 最初是一款残酷无情、赢家通吃的 PvEvP 体验。我们希望在为喜欢这种体验的玩家保留这份刺激的同时，也为其他人创造更多方式来体验这个世界，并找到适合他们的挑战难度。

免责声明：以上是即将推出的 Symbiosis CG 预告片的一帧画面。

有些玩家想在下班后快速来一局充满 较低 压力的行动。有些人想和队友通宵游玩。还有人想测试配装、与其他玩家正面对决、追求高难度挑战，或循着奇怪的线索深入 Tau Ceti IV 的废墟。

Marathon 需要满足所有这些需求。

随着我们从 Symbiosis 迈向三月及更远的未来，我们聚焦于三个领域。

更多玩法

PvEvP 仍将是 Marathon 的重要组成部分，但它会成为一整套相互关联的生存体验中的一环。

我们正在朝着以下方向构建：

  • 相互关联的 PvE、PvP 和 PvEvP 体验
  • 不同层次的强度与投入程度
  • 为独行玩家和团队提供选择
  • 更易上手的入门与学习途径
  • 围绕合作、竞争和高风险生存构建的体验

更易上手的入门途径将帮助新玩家和回归玩家理解 Marathon 的系统，并在迎接最高风险挑战前建立信心。与此同时，经验丰富的玩家将有更多机会检验自己的精通水平，并追求更深入的挑战。

你的炮弹武器库

你将扮演一名 Runner——一名生物赛博格雇佣兵，死亡后其意识可被重新上传至新的躯壳。随着时间推移，你将打造出由躯壳组成的军械库，体现你个人的审美与游玩风格。

每一次行动都应强化适应带来的刺激感，让你变得更强、更有能力，并不断打磨自己的构筑，以当下所需的那个自己重返战斗。我们希望重新思考 Runner 与其 摇篮 的互动方式，并让你对每个躯壳的成长、能力及其在你军械库中的专精定位拥有更多掌控权。

一个吞噬你的世界

就其核心而言，Marathon 讲述的是当人类闯入未知，而未知也会予以反击时会发生什么。

Tau Ceti IV 应当更具异星感与敌意，并在我们的游戏与原版 Marathon 三部曲之间建立更紧密的联系。我们希望它的世界能奖励好奇心与冒险精神，并通过更多叙事，在多次游玩中拓展你对这个宇宙的理解。

这也意味着有更多外星人可打，以及更多值得与你的小队一起迎战的难忘遭遇。如果不能射击 Pfhor，那就不算是一款 Marathon 游戏……即便自粉丝上次见到他们以来，他们自己也经历了某种蜕变。

我们正在开发全新且更壮观的战斗单位，旨在挑战你们协同作战的方式。无论你是第一次直面某个庞然大物，还是在一次行动出岔子后杀出重围，我们都希望 Tau Ceti IV 能带来这样的体验：在你成功撤离后，你和朋友们仍会津津乐道。

而 Tau Ceti IV 仍有更多面貌等待揭晓。我们正在构建一个全新的区域，将带领 Runner 深入未知，但那里潜藏着什么，我们会留到以后再揭晓。

十月：夜幕刷新

本月早些时候，我们宣布将不再采用严格的季节性发布模式。这意味着未来更新的节奏将取决于我们正在制作的内容、工作量的大小，以及团队能够负责任地支持的范围，而不是让日程表单独决定内容何时上线。

我们将在十月初以夜幕刷新拉开序幕。这是一次聚焦式更新，旨在改进当前体验，同时团队会继续打造将于十二月和三月推出的更大规模新增内容。

关于夜幕刷新将包含哪些内容的详情：https://x.com/MarathonDevTeam/status/2100253598609535344

十二月：共生

我们迈向未来的第一个重要步骤，将随十二月的共生一同到来。

共生增加了更多游玩方式、更易上手的游戏学习途径，以及更多在较低压力环境中探索天仓五 IV 的机会。十二月引入的体验只是开始，它们将为我们提供新的基础，以便在朝着三月推进的过程中进行扩展和连接。

专门的 PvE 体验

你们已经明确表示，Marathon 需要一个让玩家可以探索、试验并迎接挑战，而无需担心被其他 Runners 猎杀的地方。

为此，Symbiosis 将引入 Marathon 的首个永久 PvE 模式。我们仍在最终确定该体验的部分内容，并将在更新上线前分享更深入的介绍。

全新的竞技方式

Symbiosis 还将引入一个实验性的团队死亡竞赛模式。这种体验提供了一种更直接、较低-投入度的方式来磨练你的技能并参与竞技，而无需让一次完整行动面临风险。我们将观察玩家如何参与这项实验，并利用我们学到的东西来帮助塑造 Marathon 竞技玩法的未来。

扩展后的 Perimeter

自上线以来，Perimeter 一直是新 Runners 的入门区域，但它一直缺乏我们其他区域那种鲜明的特色和深度。

在 Symbiosis 中，Perimeter 经历了重大的视觉翻新。新的真菌生态系统正在地貌上蔓延，改变着熟悉的空间，开辟新路线，并赋予该区域独特的观感与氛围，使其有别于我们的其他区域。

Perimeter 也一直缺少像 前哨 上的 Pinwheel 和 Night Marsh 上的 Upper Complex 这类地点所提供的体验——这些体验给玩家带来有意义的挑战去克服，以及可获取的高品阶战利品。

“共生”通过新的兴趣点和富有挑战性的可攀登山峰，将同样的探索与成就感带到 Perimeter，其中包括一场巅峰遭遇战，登场的是我们迄今为止体型最大的战斗单位：蜱虫女王。

全新机体

就机体类型而言，我们还将为你的阵容添加一种新类型。Bishop：Marathon的高性能机器人武士。

Bishop 是一台快速、精准的猎杀机器，围绕速度、突袭和毁灭性的近战爆发伤害打造。我们期待看到玩家自己去发现，这种玩法幻想如何融入你的队伍，以及 Bishop 能在 Marathon 的不同体验中扮演怎样的角色。

更好的 Marathon 入门方式

目前，Marathon 要求新玩家在太短的时间内学习太多内容。

我们正在改进新手引导体验，为进入 Marathon 的世界打造更平滑的入口，让新玩家能够更轻松地学习基础知识、建立信心，然后再深入 Tau Ceti IV。

作为这项工作的一部分，我们还将添加一个由 CyAc 赞助的 社交 空间，其中内置了射击场，玩家可以在这里会面、做准备、测试武器和能力，并在 ONI 的注视下熟悉自己的机体。

三月：Marathon 的进化

在三月，这些内容将汇聚在一起，成为 Marathon 更宏大进化的一部分。

本次更新的计划包括：

  • 一个由永久 PvE、PvP 和 PvEvP 体验构成的互联系统
  • 全新的新手引导体验
  • 重构后的 摇篮 以及更深入的 shell 进阶
  • 更聚焦的任务线与叙事
  • 新的外星战斗单位
  • 一个全新的区域

三月所代表的，是远比 Symbiosis 更具分量的东西，但这也不是 Marathon 演进的终点。制作一款持续运营游戏最大的快乐，就是与你们——我们的玩家——一同将它发展下去。三月正是我们所描述的体验开始作为一个相互连接的整体运作的节点，让我们拥有更坚实的基础，在 2027 年之后继续建设。

而我们将携手完成。

有意为之的进化

我们知道，重建信任需要的不仅仅是对未来的谈论。我们需要分享我们的工作，并清楚地说明正在发生的变化，让 Marathon 社区准确理解每次更新会带来什么、不会带来什么。

在我们朝着十二月和三月推进开发的同时，我们会继续带来有意义的体验优化，并开展目标明确的实验，让玩家不必围绕有限的时间窗口重新安排生活，也能参与其中。

与这篇博客一同，我们还发布了一支新的 Dev Insights 视频，更近距离地审视我们正在应对的一些挑战，以及我们正在构建的空间。我们没有选择发布传统的开发日志，而是想让大家坦诚地看到我们正在尝试解决哪些问题，以及在工作室内部工作逐步成形之际，哪些决策正在塑造我们的方向。

我们仍有很多工作要做，随着体验的各个部分逐渐成形，我们也会有更多内容可以分享。

目前，感谢你们的游玩，感谢你们清晰而充满热情的反馈，也感谢你们在我们把 Marathon 打造成我们知道它能够成为的游戏的过程中一直陪伴着我们。

-Del 和 Julia
