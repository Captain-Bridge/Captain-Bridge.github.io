Marathon Update 1.1.9.5

The one about Vault Breaker and C.A.R.R.I. returning

Marathon Dev Team October 20, 2026
community278 words1 min read

Events

Vault Breaker

  • Vault Breaker will be available from October 20 – November 10.
  • Vault Breaker Armory:
    • Tier 1 Repeatable Offers
      • Materials Pack 1: Stock decreased from 5/week to 2/week.
      • Materials Pack 2: Stock decreased from 5/week to 2/week.
      • Cryo Archive Key Template: Stock decreased from 3/week to 1/week.
      • Deluxe weapon: Stock decreased from 3/week to 2/week.
      • Deluxe Backpack: Stock decreased from 3/week to 2/week.
      • Deluxe Shield Implant: Stock decreased from 3/week to 2/week.
    • Tier 2 Repeatable Offers:
      • Vault Key 4, 5, and 6: Only available for purchase during the 2nd and 3rd weeks of Vault Breaker being active.
    • Tier 2 Limited Offers that will only be available during the 2nd and 3rd weeks of Vault Breaker being active:
      • DRRVISH
      • Salt Wages
      • Omnipositor
      • Biotoxic Disinjector
      • Biotoxic Disinjector Ammo
    • Upgrades
      • All upgrades will be available for purchase from the start.
    • Econ Changes
      • Solo players will receive 50% more Tier 1 and Tier 2 event currency from Vault Breaker.
      • Monitor scan XP has been reduced by 50%.
      • A selection of Tier 3 standard contracts are not intended to be completed during Vault Breaker and will not be available during the event.

C.A.R.R.I.

  • C.A.R.R.I. Armory has returned and will run through the end of Nightfall refresh.

Sponsored Night Marsh

  • Sponsored Night Marsh will be available this weekend, starting Thursday, 10/22.

Weapons

  • Updated the description of the Repeater HPR to correctly state it reloads 2 rounds at a time.

Codex

  • Fixed an issue with the timing requirements on the Recon Trifecta Mastery challenge.

General

  • Fixed an issue in profile stats where Global Exfils would appear different when previewing different seasons.
  • Fixed an issue where the profile stats page could sometimes appear blank.

活动

破库者

  • 破库者将于10月21日至11月11日期间开放。
  • 破库者军械库：
    • 1阶可重复优惠
      • 材料包1：库存从每周5件减少到每周2件。
      • 材料包2：库存从每周5件减少到每周2件。
      • 低温档案室钥匙模板：库存从每周3件减少到每周1件。
      • 豪华武器：库存从每周3件减少到每周2件。
      • 豪华背包：库存从每周3件减少到每周2件。
      • 豪华护盾植入物：库存从每周3件减少到每周2件。
    • 2阶可重复优惠：
      • 保险库钥匙4、5和6：仅在破库者上线的第2周和第3周开放购买。
    • 仅在破库者上线的第2周和第3周开放购买的2阶限定优惠：
      • DRRVISH
      • 苦涩报酬
      • 全向器
      • 生物毒素分离注射器
      • 生物毒素分离注射器弹药
    • 升级
      • 所有升级从开始时即可购买。
    • 经济改动
      • 单人玩家在破库者中获得的1阶和2阶活动货币数量将增加50%。
      • 监控扫描获得的经验值降低了50%。
      • 部分3阶标准合约并非在破库者期间完成，因此活动期间将无法进行。

C.A.R.R.I.

  • C.A.R.R.I.军械库现已回归，并持续至暮夜刷新结束。

赞助夜间沼泽

  • 赞助夜间沼泽将于本周末上线，10月23日周五开始。

武器

  • 更新了连击者重型精密步枪的描述，正确说明其每次填装2发弹药。

百科

  • 修复了侦察三连胜精通挑战中关于时机要求的问题。

综合

  • 修复了在档案数据统计中，预览不同赛季时，全局撤离显示不一致的问题。
  • 修复了档案数据统计页面有时可能显示为空白的问题。
