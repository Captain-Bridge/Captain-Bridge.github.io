Marathon Symbiosis: Open Play Week and Crew Up Event

Play with your friends during Open Play Week

Marathon Dev Team November 10, 2026
community718 words3 min read

The evolution of Marathon starts on December 8 with the launch of our next update, Symbiosis, which will bring a plethora of updates and changes to our game, including phase one of our permanent PvE game mode. So, in order for everyone to have a chance to experience first-hand how Marathon has grown and improved, we are opening the game to everyone during our Open Play Week.

Let's talk about it!

Everyone Can Play During Open Play Week 

Just like we did at the start of Season 2, we want everyone to experience the new content coming to Marathon. It will be the perfect time to invite your friends not only to try the game, but to also help you get some Crew Up rewards.

During the Open Play Week from December 8 until December 15, players will be able to access and experience everything Symbiosis has to offer, including the new Expedition PvE game mode, the new Bishop shell, the updated Perimeter map, and all the new gear and rewards. If you choose to purchase Marathon after Open Play Week is over, you will keep all the items and progression unlocked.

Crew Up Event: Play Together, Win Together

Crew Up is an event with a clear goal: form a team with your friends and explore Tau Ceti IV with a more collaborative mindset in the new Expedition PvE game mode. The reward: all the gear you can get on your runs, plus some unique cosmetics on top.

Completing consecutive challenges during the Crew Up event will unlock new Codex entries, which will allow each member of the team to get some new cosmetic rewards:

  • Emblem: Queen's Nest
  • Background: Queen's Dominion
  • Weapon sticker: Queen's Maw
  • Weapon charm: Cluster Bunch
  • Destroyer skin: Arata Vectus
  • Title: Exterminator

Crew Up starts on Symbiosis launch date, December 8, at 12PM PT and will be available through December 22, 10am PT. During its first week, we will be running an Open Play Week, so even your friends who don't yet own Marathon can join your efforts and participate in Crew Up at no additional cost. 

How To Participate

Players looking to participate in Crew Up need to redeem the following code on bungie.net/redeem when Symbiosis is live on December 8. Don't worry, we'll remind you closer to that date! 

- DMA-T7Y-XJL -

Once the code has been redeemed, a new Events section in the in-game Codex will open up and show specific Crew Up challenges that players must complete in the new PvE game mode, in Expedition. These start simple but will progressively get more complex and demanding. Good thing your friends are there to help!

One thing you need to keep in mind is that these tasks cannot be completed solo, they require that you join or create a crew with at least one other player who has redeemed the Crew Up event code. We recommend you add your crew members as Bungie friends to keep, although progression is also earned if players are on the same platform and have each other added on their proprietary friend lists. 

FAQ

Do I need to own Marathon to participate in the Crew Up Event?

During Open Play Week (December 8 to 15) everyone can download and play the full game without purchasing it. After that, and until the event finishes on December 22, players must own a Marathon license to play the event.

Can I complete the Crew Up challenges after Open Play Week ends?

Yes. Crew Up continues for an additional week after Open Play Week ends, giving you more time to complete the event objectives.

Do I need to play for a certain number of hours to access the event?

You can start completing Crew Up events after you have gone through the starting tutorial.

Does my friend need to complete every challenge?

Crew Up is built around playing together, but each player can progress and claim their own Codex rewards.

Can I play other Marathon modes with my friend during Open Play Week?

Of course! Crew Up focuses on the new Expedition PvE game mode, but you can try Extraction with your friends and earn loot, XP, and faction upgrades that way. If they buy the game after Open Play Week is over, they get to keep everything they earned, too.

公开试玩周期间每个人都可以玩

正如第2赛季初那样，我们希望每一位玩家都能体验《失落星船：马拉松》中的新内容。抓紧这次的绝佳机会，不仅可以邀请你的朋友来试玩这款游戏，还能帮你获取组队奖励。

在12月9日至12月16日的公开试玩周期间，玩家可以尽情体验共生的所有内容，包括全新远征PvE模式、全新毕肖外壳、外围区域地图更新，以及各类全新装备与奖励。若你在公开试玩周结束后选择购买《失落星船：马拉松》，此前解锁的所有物品及进度都将保留。

组队活动：并肩作战，共获胜利

组队活动的目标非常明确：与好友组建队伍，在全新远征PvE模式中，以更紧密的协作姿态共同探索天仓五IV。奖励包括：你在疾行过程中获取的所有装备，以及其他独特装饰。

在组队活动期间完成连续挑战，即可解锁新的百科条目，并让每位队员获得全新装饰奖励：

  • 徽标：女王巢穴
  • 背景：女王统治
  • 武器贴纸：女王之口
  • 武器挂件：虱虫卵群落
  • 毁灭者皮肤：阿拉塔·维克图斯
  • 称号：灭绝者

共生将于北京时间12月9日凌晨4点上线，而组队活动也将同步开启，北京时间12月22日凌晨2点结束。第一周为公开试玩周，即使你的朋友尚未拥有《失落星船：马拉松》，也能免费参与组队活动，与你携手共进。 

如何参与

若想参与组队活动，玩家需在12月9日共生上线后，前往bungie.net/redeem兑换以下代码。别担心，临近日期时我们会再次提醒你！ 

- DMA-T7Y-XJL -

兑换代码后，游戏内百科将开启新的活动部分，显示玩家须在全新PvE游戏模式远征中完成的特定组队挑战。初期较为简单，但后续会更加复杂且更具挑战性。好在有朋友可以助你一臂之力！

请注意，这些任务无法单人完成；你必须加入或组建团队，且至少需有另一名玩家同样兑换了组队活动代码。我们建议你将队员添加为Bungie好友；不过如果玩家使用同一平台且已将对方添加到各自的平台好友列表中，同样可以获得进度。 

常见问题

参与组队活动是否需要拥有《失落星船：马拉松》？

在公开试玩周（12月9日至16日）期间，任何人均可下载并游玩完整版游戏，无需购买。在此之后，直至12月23日活动结束，玩家必须拥有《失落星船：马拉松》许可才能参与该活动。

公开试玩周结束后，我还能完成组队挑战吗？

可以。在公开试玩周结束后，组队活动还会额外持续一周，让你有更多时间完成活动目标。

我需要游玩达到一定时长才能参与活动吗？

完成新手教程后，你即可开始完成组队活动。

我的好友需要完成每一项挑战吗？

组队活动的核心在于共同游玩，但每位玩家都可获得进度，并领取自己的百科奖励。

在公开试玩周期间，我可以和好友一起游玩其他《失落星船：马拉松》模式吗？

当然可以！组队活动侧重于全新远征PvE模式，但你也可以和好友一起尝试撤离，从中获取战利品、经验值以及阵营升级。如果他们在公开试玩周结束后购买了游戏，此前获得的所有奖励也都将保留。
